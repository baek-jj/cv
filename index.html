About

I am an M.S. student in the Graduate School of AI Semiconductor at KAIST, advised by Prof. Joo-Young Kim (CAST Lab). Before that I received my B.S. in Electronics Engineering from Sogang University.

My research sits between computer architecture and systems: how the memory hierarchy should be organized so that serving large language models is fast and affordable at scale.
